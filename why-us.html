Eight reasons builders specify Elephant Gypsum

Every claim below is measured and documented on our technical data sheets and ISO-certified quality system — not marketing copy.

01 · Safety

Fire Resistant & Non-Combustible

Gypsum plaster's core chemistry makes it inherently non-combustible, adding a passive fire-safety layer to walls and ceilings — valued in hospitals, hotels and high-rise residential towers.

02 · Efficiency

No Water Curing Required

Unlike cement plaster, our gypsum systems don't need days of water curing — cutting labour hours, water usage and site scheduling delays.

03 · Durability

Zero Shrinkage Cracks

Formulated to resist the inherent shrinkage cracking common with traditional cement-sand plaster, keeping walls smooth for longer.

04 · Speed

Rapid, Predictable Setting

Setting times of 12–25 minutes across our plaster range keep application crews moving through a site without long idle gaps.

05 · Comfort

Acoustic & Thermal Insulation

Gypsum plaster improves sound dampening and thermal comfort inside finished rooms compared to conventional plaster systems.

06 · Sustainability

Infinitely Recyclable

Gypsum's CaSO4 hydration cycle (+H2O / -H2O) allows the material to be re-processed indefinitely — a genuinely circular building material.

07 · Compliance

IS & ASTM Certified

Plaster products conform to IS 2547 Part 1 & 2; EGP Bond Plus is tested to ASTM D7234 and D4541 for bond strength.

08 · Reach

Proven Across 125+ Projects

Supplied to 25+ builder groups including Lodha, Godrej, Adani, Kalpataru, Prestige and Shapoorji Pallonji across Mumbai, Thane and Pune.

Backed by an ISO-certified quality system

Independently assessed by QRO Certification LLP for both quality management (ISO 9001:2015) and environmental management (ISO 14001:2015).
