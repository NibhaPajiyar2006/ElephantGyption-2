What each standard means for your project

Two complementary systems that govern how we manufacture, test and ship every batch.

Quality

ISO 9001:2015

A globally recognised quality management standard. It requires documented, repeatable processes for production, testing and corrective action — so every bag or bucket that leaves our Bikaner facility meets the same specification, batch after batch.

Environment

ISO 14001:2015

An environmental management standard covering resource use, waste handling and environmental risk across manufacturing. It formalises our commitment to responsible production alongside gypsum's naturally recyclable material cycle.
