Residential Towers & Townships

High-rise and township builds need predictable setting times to keep floor-by-floor schedules moving, plus a smooth, crack-free finish for handover.

Non-combustible, fire-resistant walls and ceilings add a passive safety layer that healthcare projects specifically look for in plaster systems.
