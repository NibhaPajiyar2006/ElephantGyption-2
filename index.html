Company Overview

A trusted manufacturer of gypsum-based construction materials

Elephant Gypsum Private Limited designs high-performance gypsum plaster systems that help builders achieve superior interior finishes while improving construction efficiency, from our factory in Bikaner, Rajasthan.

Our vision is to become one of the most respected gypsum plaster manufacturers in India by delivering innovative products, consistent quality, and dependable service to the construction industry.
