Single Coat N1

A hemi-hydrate gypsum plaster, completely made in India, designed for internal wall and ceiling applications in a single coat at regular thicknesses. The formulation incorporates lightweight vermiculite aggregates to enhance workability, improve coverage, and provide controlled setting characteristics.

Overview & Advantages
  • Fire resistant & non-combustible
  • Good acoustic and thermal insulation
  • No water curing required
  • Gains early strength, no shrinkage cracks
  • Infinite recycling — green & sustainable
  • Sets in less than 30 minutes
Applications
  • Internal wall finishing
  • Ceiling applications
  • Residential & commercial interiors
Packaging & Storage
  • Packed in 25 kg BOPP bags, batch-coded
  • Stack 150–200mm off the floor, 600mm from walls
  • Max stack height 10 bags — store dry & leak-proof (IS 4082)

Bonding Agent · ASTM D7234 / D4541

EGP Bond Plus

A distinctive bonding agent that excels on RCC surfaces lined with gypsum plaster. Its coarse texture and fine aggregates deliver robust adhesion without labour-intensive surface preparation, hacking, or other prep methods.

Overview & Advantages
  • Ready to apply in a single coat
  • Green colour for effortless identification on site
  • Enriched with fine aggregates for mechanical & chemical adhesion
  • No hacking required on the RCC surface
  • Swift, cost-effective application — saves time & money
Applications
  • Pre-treating smooth RCC surfaces before plastering
  • Bonding agent ahead of gypsum plaster application
Packaging & Storage
  • Packed in plastic buckets, labelled with batch & date
  • Stack securely on pallets, store cool & dry
  • Use promptly once opened
