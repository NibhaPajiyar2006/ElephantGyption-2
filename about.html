Company Overview

Who we are

Elephant Gypsum Private Limited manufactures and supplies gypsum-based construction materials designed for modern building practices. The company focuses on delivering high-performance gypsum plaster systems that help builders achieve superior interior finishes while improving construction efficiency.

Our registered office is in Thane, Maharashtra, with our manufacturing facility located in Bikaner, Rajasthan — the heart of India's natural gypsum belt. From there we supply plaster and bonding systems to builder groups across Mumbai, Thane and Pune.

Vision

To become one of the most respected gypsum plaster manufacturers in India by delivering innovative products, consistent quality and dependable service to the construction industry.

Mission

Provide high-quality gypsum plaster solutions, support builders with reliable product performance, and continuously improve our product technology.

Our entire product range is built on this natural, infinitely recyclable hydration cycle.

Our journey

Key milestones in building Elephant Gypsum's manufacturing and quality credentials.

Foundation

Elephant Gypsum Private Limited established

Founded with a focus on gypsum-based plaster and bonding agents for the Indian construction market, headquartered in Thane, Maharashtra.

Manufacturing

Bikaner manufacturing facility commissioned

Production base established at Sarah Jodki, Khari Charanan, Sri Kolayat, Bikaner, Rajasthan — close to natural gypsum reserves.

Product Range

Four-product portfolio launched

Single Coat N1, Single Coat N1 Plus, Single Coat Unique N2 and EGP Bond Plus brought to market, each conforming to IS 2547.

26 June 2026

ISO 9001:2015 & ISO 14001:2015 certified

Independently assessed and certified by QRO Certification LLP for Quality Management and Environmental Management systems, valid to 25 June 2029.

Today

125+ projects, 25+ builder partners

Supplying leading developers including Lodha, Godrej, Adani, Kalpataru, Prestige and Shapoorji Pallonji across Mumbai, Thane and Pune.

Manufacturing & quality assurance

Our Bikaner facility is independently audited for both product quality and environmental practice.

Scope

Manufacturing of gypsum plaster & bonding agent

Certified scope covers our full product line — from raw gypsum processing to finished, packed goods.
